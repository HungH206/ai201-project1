University of Houston Meal Plan Eligibility Reference

The University of Houston offers meal plan options for both residential students and commuter students. Residential students living in Cougar Village I, Cougar Village II, Cougar Place, Moody Towers, and The Quad are required to have a mandatory residential meal plan, except for The Quad Townhouses.

Commuter students can use non-residential options such as Block meal plans and Cougar Dining Dollars plans. Block 100, Block 50, and Cougar Dining Dollars plans are examples of meal plan choices that may work for students who do not live on campus.

Meal plan selections are managed through AccessUH using Cougar Card Connect. Meal plan fees are determined by semester, and residential meal plan selections can carry over from fall to spring unless changed or canceled according to UH meal plan rules.
