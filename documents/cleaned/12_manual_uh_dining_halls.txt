University of Houston Dining Halls Reference

The University of Houston has dining halls and dining commons for students. The main dining halls mentioned in UH campus food information include Moody Towers Dining Commons and Cougar Woods Dining Commons.

UH campus dining also includes retail food locations, restaurants, coffee shops, and Student Center food options. Dining halls are different from retail restaurants because dining halls usually support meal plan use and all-you-care-to-eat dining.
