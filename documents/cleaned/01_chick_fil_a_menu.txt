How to order Chick-fil-A Menu?

Ordering from the Chick-fil-A menu is easy and convenient, whether you’re dining in, picking up, or getting delivery. Here are six simple ways to place your order and enjoy your favorite meals fast:

1. In-Store Ordering

Visit your nearest Chick-fil-A restaurant, browse the menu, and order directly at the counter. You can view prices, customize your meal, and enjoy it fresh and hot in minutes.

2. Drive-Thru

Short on time? The Chick-fil-A Drive-Thru lets you order your favorite menu items without leaving your car. Simply pull up, order through the speaker, and pick up your meal at the window.

3. Chick-fil-A Mobile App

The Chick-fil-A App makes ordering even easier. Download it to browse the full Chick-fil-A menu with prices, customize your order, earn rewards, and choose pickup or delivery — all from your phone.

4. Online Ordering

You can also place your order directly on the official Chick-fil-A website. Log in, select your location, explore the menu, and pay securely online. It’s perfect for planning ahead or office lunches.

5. Third-Party Delivery

Prefer delivery? Order from the Chick-fil-A menu through apps like DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub to enjoy your favorites right at your doorstep.

6. Call-In Orders

Some locations still accept phone orders. Just call your local Chick-fil-A, place your order, and pick it up at your preferred time.

💡 Pro Tip:The fastest way to order from the Chick-fil-A menu is through the mobile app, where you can earn rewards, track orders, and get exclusive offers.

Frequently Asked Questions About Chick-fil-A Menu

What time does Chick-fil-A stop serving breakfast?

Most Chick-fil-A restaurants serve breakfast from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM, Monday through Saturday. After 10:30 AM, the lunch menu begins.

Does Chick-fil-A serve lunch all day?

No, lunch starts right after breakfast ends, typically from 10:30 AM until closing hours. You can enjoy all-day favorites from the Chick-fil-A menu, including sandwiches, nuggets, and sides. See our complete guide to Chick-fil-A lunch hours.

Does Chick-fil-A take Apple Pay?

Yes, Chick-fil-A accepts Apple Pay at all locations — both in-store and drive-thru. You can also use Apple Pay for orders placed through the Chick-fil-A App.

Is Chick-fil-A Halal or Kosher?

Currently, Chick-fil-A is not certified Halal or Kosher in the United States. However, customers looking for specific dietary options can check the Chick-fil-A allergen and ingredient menu for full details.

Is Chick-fil-A gluten-free?

Chick-fil-A offers some gluten-free menu options, such as the Grilled Nuggets and gluten-free bun. You can find complete allergen and ingredient information in the Chick-fil-A Nutrition Guide.

What are the most popular items on the Chick-fil-A menu?

Customer favorites include the Original Chicken Sandwich, Chick-fil-A Nuggets, Mac & Cheese, and the Cookies & Cream Milkshake.

Does Chick-fil-A have milkshakes?

Yes! The Chick-fil-A Milkshake Menu includes delicious flavors like Cookies & Cream, Chocolate, Vanilla, and Strawberry, all made with Chick-fil-A’s creamy Icedream® dessert.

How can I check my Chick-fil-A gift card balance?

You can check your Chick-fil-A gift card balance online at chick-fil-a.com, through the Chick-fil-A App, or by calling 1-888-232-1864.

What sauces does Chick-fil-A have?

The Chick-fil-A sauces menu includes favorites like Chick-fil-A Sauce, Polynesian Sauce, Honey Mustard, Barbecue, and Garden Herb Ranch — all available in restaurants and 8oz bottles.

Does Chick-fil-A offer catering?

Yes, the Chick-fil-A catering menu includes nugget trays, chick-n-strips, wrap trays, salads, and breakfast trays, perfect for family events or business meetings.

How many calories are in Chick-fil-A meals?

Calories vary depending on your order — for example, a Chick-fil-A Chicken Sandwich has about 420 calories, and a Cookies & Cream Milkshake has around 630 calories. Check the Chick-fil-A Nutrition Menu for full calorie details.

Does Chick-fil-A offer any deals or promo codes?

Yes! You can find exclusive Chick-fil-A deals, rewards, and coupons inside the Chick-fil-A One® App, where members earn free food and special offers all year long.

Does Chick-fil-A serve breakfast all day?

No. Chick-fil-A serves breakfast only until 10:30 AM. After that, only lunch and dinner items are available.
