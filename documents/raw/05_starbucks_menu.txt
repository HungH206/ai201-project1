How to Order from Starbucks

Quick size guide

Tall 12 oz. Grande 16 oz. Venti Hot 20 oz. Venti Cold 24 oz. Trenta 31 oz for iced drinks.

Step-by-step (in-store or drive-thru)



Pick your drink type. Hot coffee, espresso, cold brew, iced coffee, tea, refreshers, Frappuccino.

Choose hot, iced, or blended.

Choose size. Tall, Grande, Venti, Trenta for iced.

Pick roast or strength. Blonde, signature, or decaf. Add shots if needed.

Choose milk. Whole, 2%, nonfat, oat, almond, soy, coconut, breve.

Set sweetness. Syrups and sauces. Vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, mocha, white chocolate. Say number of pumps. Sugar-free if you want.

Ice and temperature. Light ice, no ice, extra hot, kid temp.

Toppings. Whipped cream, cold foam, caramel drizzle, mocha drizzle, cinnamon or cocoa.

Add food. Breakfast sandwich, bakery, protein box, wrap, panini.

Give your name. Check the order on the screen. Pay and collect.



Step-by-step (mobile order)



Open the Starbucks app. Select store and pickup method.

Pick a menu category.

Choose size. Tap Customize.

Set shots, milk, syrups, pumps, toppings, ice.

Add food items.

Pay with Starbucks Card or saved payment.

Watch status. Pick up from the Mobile Order shelf.



Order templates



“Grande Iced Caramel Macchiato, oat milk, half vanilla, light ice, caramel drizzle.”

“Tall Pike Place, room for cream.”

“Venti Cold Brew, two pumps vanilla, splash of oat milk, light ice.”

“Grande Matcha Latte, almond milk, two scoops matcha, less sweet.”



Pro tips

Confirm size, temperature, and milk. Say the number of syrup pumps. For the secret menu, give the full recipe. Check you selected the right store in the app. Scan your Starbucks Rewards card to earn Stars. History of Starbucks – From Seattle Roots to Global Coffee Culture

Starbucks began in 1971 at Seattle’s Pike Place Market. Three friends, Jerry Baldwin, Zev Siegl, and Gordon Bowker, opened a small shop selling fresh roasted coffee beans and equipment. The name came from the classic novel Moby-Dick, symbolizing seafaring tradition and coffee’s global journey.

In 1982, Howard Schultz joined and envisioned a café model inspired by Italian espresso bars. By 1987, Starbucks started expanding beyond Seattle, offering handcrafted espresso drinks. Rapid growth in the 1990s brought stores across the United States, a public stock listing, and the birth of the iconic green siren logo.

The 2000s saw global expansion—Asia, Europe, and the Middle East—while adding Frappuccino, seasonal drinks like the Pumpkin Spice Latte, and a strong Starbucks Rewards program. Today, with 30,000+ stores worldwide, Starbucks blends premium coffee, ethical sourcing, and café culture, shaping how millions enjoy coffee every day.
