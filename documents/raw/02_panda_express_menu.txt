Panda Express Menu Allergen & Dietary Information:

Since Panda Express Menu Prices 2026 operates in shared kitchens, cross-contact risks are important to consider.

Allergen Notes:


Gluten: No certified gluten-free dishes. Chow Mein, soy sauces, and breaded proteins contain gluten.

Dairy: Most entrées are dairy-free, but Cream Cheese Rangoons contain dairy.

Eggs: Fried Rice and egg rolls contain eggs.

Shellfish: Honey Walnut Shrimp contains shellfish; cross-contact possible.

Peanuts: Kung Pao Chicken includes peanuts.


Vegetarian & Vegan Options:


Some locations offer Eggplant Tofu, but it’s not guaranteed everywhere.

Super Greens (broccoli, kale, cabbage) are vegan-friendly.

Steamed White Rice is another vegan option.

No fully certified vegan or vegetarian meals due to shared cooking surfaces.

Is Panda Express Healthy?



Panda Express meals can be part of a balanced diet if chosen wisely, but many menu items are high in calories, sodium, and sugar. Healthier choices include Wok Smart entrées like String Bean Chicken Breast, Broccoli Beef, or Kung Pao Chicken, which have less than 300 calories per serving and more vegetables. Limiting fried items and sugary sauces helps make your meal more nutritious.





Does Panda Express Take Apple Pay?



Yes, Panda Express accepts Apple Pay at most of its locations across the U.S. You can use Apple Pay for in-store, drive-thru, and online orders through the Panda Express app or compatible delivery platforms. It’s a fast, secure, and contactless payment option.





Who Owns Panda Express?



Panda Express is owned by Panda Restaurant Group, Inc., which was founded by Andrew Cherng and Peggy Cherng in 1983. The company is privately held and operates several restaurant brands, including Panda Inn and Hibachi-San. The Cherng family still owns and manages the brand today.





Does Panda Express Use MSG?



Panda Express states that it does not add MSG (monosodium glutamate) to any of its dishes during preparation. However, some ingredients may naturally contain MSG from suppliers, especially soy sauce, chicken stock, or seasoning mixes. So, while no extra MSG is added, trace amounts may still be present.
